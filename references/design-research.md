## How to research

1. Understand the project and identify what needs inspiration.
2. Search across multiple sources and categories.
3. Inspect actual websites when possible.
4. Capture specific observations: typography, composition, navigation, transitions, product storytelling, or responsive behavior.
5. Explain why each observation is relevant.
6. Adapt the underlying idea to the project's brand and content.
7. Avoid copying a site's complete composition, distinctive assets, or identity.

## Reference evaluation

For each useful reference, consider:
- What makes it memorable?
- What does it communicate especially well?
- Which design technique is worth learning from?
- Would that technique fit this project's audience and product?
- How could it be adapted without imitation?

## Research integrity

Do not claim to have visited or inspected a page if browser access was unavailable. Search snippets and thumbnails are not equivalent to inspecting the live experience. Do not reproduce copyrighted assets or distinctive site designs wholesale.
